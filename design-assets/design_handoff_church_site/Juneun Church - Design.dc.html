01 · BRAND

브리즈번 주는교회 — Brand Foundations

A modern rebuild of the identity, moving off the bright green to a deep navy that reads trustworthy and established. Built on the Modernist system — flat, architectural, a visible grid, 2 px rules, and a single sparing blue accent — carrying the clean, statement-driven structure of the Rothem Tree reference into a Korean-primary church site.

The mark

A clean, bold cross — the church’s own mark — set beside the Korean wordmark 브리즈번 주는교회 with the denomination 대한예수교 장로회 directly beneath. Simple, warm and modern; it works in navy on light, reversed on navy, and in gold.
